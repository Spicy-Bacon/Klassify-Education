# MVP Roadmap Notes

The foundation stage should stay aligned with Phase 1 product needs without implementing later modules prematurely.

```text
Phase 1 - Identity & School Structure         Complete
Phase 2 - Admin Portal Core                   Complete
Phase 3 - Announcements                       Complete

```

## Next Product Stage

## Future MVP Areas

- Calendar & Events.
- AI Writing Assistance.
- Staff App.
- Attendance.
- Leave Requests.
- Media / Galleries.
- Consent Centre.
- School AI.
- Messaging.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Payments.
- Accounting.
- Full grading or report cards.
- Complex timetable generation.
- Full LMS functionality.
- Admissions system.
- Automatic student facial recognition.
- Livestreaming.
- Advanced video generation.
- Full student generative AI.
- Cross-school analytics.
- Large third-party integration frameworks.

The product should not become an eClass clone.
