# MVP Roadmap Notes

The foundation stage should stay aligned with Phase 1 product needs without implementing later modules prematurely.

## Current Completed Foundations

- Identity and School Structure: Complete.
- Admin Portal Core: Complete.
- Announcements: Complete.
- Parent App V1: Complete.
- Forms / Digital Reply Slips: Complete once PR #5 is approved and merged.

## Future MVP Areas

- Connect: notifications, calendar and basic messaging remain future work after forms and reply slips.
- Staff experience: attendance, announcements, messages, forms, calendar, AI drafting and photo upload.
- Parent experience: calendar, messaging, leave request, multi-child support expansion and private galleries remain future work after Parent App V1 and Forms.
- Student experience: notices, calendar, homework or tasks and resources.
- Media Suite: private galleries, event organization, controlled access, consent records and basic photo selection or ordering.
- AI: notice drafting, translation assistance, summarization and authorized school-document Q&A.
- Admin Web: deeper configuration, calendar, responses, permissions and media administration.

## Next Product Stage

- Admin Portal operational shell, followed by the first complete Announcements workflow expansion.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Payments.
- Accounting.
- Full grading or report cards.
- Complex timetable generation.
- Full LMS functionality.
- Admissions system.
- Automatic student facial recognition.
- Livestreaming.
- Advanced video generation.
- Full student generative AI.
- Cross-school analytics.
- Large third-party integration frameworks.

The product should not become an eClass clone.
